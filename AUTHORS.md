## Disclosure policy

Future pull requests should identify the human contributors and disclose which
AI systems materially assisted the change. Only systems that actually
contributed to that pull request should be selected in its disclosure.

Git commits retain the identity of their human contributor. Do not create
synthetic email addresses or `Co-authored-by` identities for AI systems.

Earlier changes were created before model-level disclosure was part of the
project workflow. Their published history will not be rewritten. The
project-level acknowledgement above records the AI assistance used during that
period without assigning unverifiable model participation to individual commits.
